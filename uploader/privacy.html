Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy covers Channel Uploader ("the app"), a private tool the channel owner uses to publish their own videos to their own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): with the owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channel and to read basic information about that channel's own videos, such as titles and view counts.
  • TikTok: with the owner's permission, the app uses TikTok's Content Posting API to publish videos to the owner's TikTok account and reads that account's basic profile information.

What the app stores

The app stores the sign-in tokens these services issue, on the owner's own computer only, so it can keep posting. It keeps a local list of the videos it has posted and their view counts. It does not store, sell or share any information about other people, and it has no other users.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Videos are sent only to the platform they are posted on.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google's security settings, from TikTok's "Manage app permissions", or by emailing us; the stored tokens are then deleted.
